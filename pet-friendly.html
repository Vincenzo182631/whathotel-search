Pet-Friendly Luxury Hotels

Your dog or cat doesn't have to stay home. These 751 luxury hotels welcome pets, from Kimpton and Thompson to Four Seasons, The Ritz-Carlton, St. Regis, Park Hyatt and independent country houses across Europe. You'll find them in more than 60 countries, with the most in New York, San Francisco, Paris, London, Chicago, Washington DC, Rome and Milan.

Book through WhataHotel! and you pay the hotel's own rate, with our preferred-partner perks added where the hotel offers them. Pet rules are set by each hotel, and they change. Before you book, a Lorraine Travel advisor will confirm the details for your pet: the fee, the weight limit, how many pets are allowed and which room types qualify.

Frequently asked questions

How many pet-friendly hotels does WhataHotel! offer?

751 luxury hotels in 65 countries welcome pets, from Kimpton and Thompson to Four Seasons, The Ritz-Carlton, St. Regis, Park Hyatt and independent country houses across Europe.

Who sets the pet rules, and how do I know they apply to my pet?

Pet rules are set by each hotel, and they change. Before you book, a Lorraine Travel advisor will confirm the details for your pet: the fee, the weight limit, how many pets are allowed and which room types qualify.

What rate do I pay when I book through WhataHotel!?

You pay the hotel's own rate, with WhataHotel!'s preferred-partner perks added where the hotel offers them.

Are there pet-friendly villas and residences?

Yes. 62 villas, residences and homes in the WhataHotel! Homes collection welcome pets. Each shows a Pet-friendly tag and its pet policy on the Homes page.

How do I book a pet-friendly hotel?

Find the hotel on this page and select View Rates to see live rates for your dates on WhataHotel.com, or contact an advisor at Reservations@WhataHotel.com or +1-877-782-9281.
