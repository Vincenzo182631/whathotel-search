Good to know: Exceptional Homes Q&A

What's included with a Mandarin Oriental Exceptional Home?

Every rental includes a dedicated concierge to assist with pre-arrival requests, local experiences and transportation; daily housekeeping inspired by Mandarin Oriental's exacting standards; a full-time Villa, Property or Estate Manager; special arrangements for children of all ages; and a $500 Concierge Credit (the equivalent of US$500, applied in the property's local currency), which can be used toward grocery provisioning, in-villa spa treatments and more.

Is there a dedicated chef available?

Not every home includes a chef service; however, for properties where a chef is not included, we can arrange one at an additional cost. Whether included in the villa rate or booked separately, all chef services are approved by Mandarin Oriental. Most properties charge separately for food and beverage provisions, although a select number of homes include these costs in the rate.

Are airport transfers included?

It varies by home. A dedicated concierge team can arrange all transfers at an additional cost. Some properties include transfers as part of the villa rate; this is detailed within each property's inclusions.

What other amenities are provided?

Mandarin Oriental robes, slippers and luxury toiletries are provided in all homes.

Frequently asked questions

What is a hotel residence, villa or home-style stay?

It's a private residence, villa or home at a hotel or resort — with separate bedrooms and living areas, and often a full kitchen, dining area and laundry — plus the hotel's service, such as housekeeping, dining, spas and amenities. It gives families, groups and longer stays far more space than a single hotel room.

How many properties are in the WhataHotel! Homes collection?

376 in total: 334 hotels and resorts with villas, residences and homes, plus 42 Mandarin Oriental Exceptional Homes. By region: North America 94, Latin America & Caribbean 89, Europe 96, Asia 57, Middle East & Africa 25, Pacific & Indian Ocean 15.

Which hotel brands are included?

Brands include Mandarin Oriental, Signature Collection, Four Seasons, Hyatt, Leading Hotels of the World, Ritz-Carlton, Preferred, Rosewood, Auberge Resorts and Luxury Collection, among others. Every property shows its brand or collection on its card.

How do I book a villa or residence?

Search by destination, open a property and select Check Availability. You'll see live availability on the property's WhataHotel! page, filtered to its residential-style accommodation (villas, residences and homes), then choose the one that suits your trip.

What are the Mandarin Oriental Exceptional Homes?

They are 42 private villas and estates from Mandarin Oriental's Exceptional Homes collection, in Indonesia, Thailand, United Kingdom, Italy, France, Spain, Greece and Croatia. Each home lists its bedrooms, bathrooms, guest capacity and an indicative starting rate where Mandarin Oriental publishes one.

Can I book a Mandarin Oriental Exceptional Home online?

No. These homes are not bookable online. Select Request Availability, share your preferred dates and party size, and a WhataHotel! travel advisor will contact you about availability and rates. Submitting a request does not confirm a reservation, and published rates are indicative and subject to confirmation.

Are these homes good for families and groups?

Yes. With multiple bedrooms and separate living and dining areas, everyone can stay together in one home instead of booking several hotel rooms. Guest capacity varies by property.
